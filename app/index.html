Roadmap to the platinum

Three steps, in order. The counts under each step come from the official trophy descriptions; the times and the step order follow PowerPyx’s roadmap and GTABase’s trophy guide (see Sources below).

  1. 1

    Play the story — 60–80 hours

    Play all five chapters and take optional missions as they appear. Scan vehicles with the WAiNK app and study animals with binoculars as you travel: that progress already counts toward 100% later. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation (see the warning above). Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter — there is no mission replay. Never use cheats: they disable trophies and saving. Avoid the permanent Irredeemable criminal profile.

  2. 2

    The 100% checklist — about 100 hours

    After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion and work through it. It is lenient — closer to 60% true completion. Needed in full: every robbery, all 8 bank vaults, all 39 gang operations, all underwater discoveries, all 42 shooting-range gold medals, all drone races and all 15 of Bret’s Target Decks. Needed in part: 100 optional missions, 200 of 277 vehicle scans, 100 of 255 animal studies, 15 of 18 meditations, 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts, 100 WAiNK scans.

  3. 3

    Strangers & Freaks and cleanup — about 10 hours

    Finish the 8 qualifying Strangers & Freaks strands (Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete count; Moses, Tyrone, Yasmani, Andy, Billy and Eddie do not), any character questlines still open, then sweep the remaining miscellaneous trophies below.
